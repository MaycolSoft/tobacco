Object study, suspended abstract assemblage, horizontal composition. Three thin, gently curled sheets of real cured tobacco leaf, matte and fibrous in browns #5E3F28 to #7A5236, each half rolled along its length, floating parallel to each other along a horizontal axis with small gaps between them. Around them, a single narrow ribbon of translucent resin with embedded fibers makes one loose open spiral turn, its depth glowing resin amber #D4913F where backlight passes through. Outside that, a wider opaque ribbon of satin cured wrapper leaf in colorado brown #8A5A3B winds around the bundle in a loose helix, leaving clear gaps so the inner sheets remain visible. A single tiny claret #7B2F2B glint where the outer ribbon closes on itself. Both ends of the assemblage stay open, ragged and airy, like an abstract cocoon; it must never read as a cigar. Warm side key light, soft backlight through the resin, faint shadow. Background: warm dark card #171512. Series style: quiet luxury editorial still life, Tabacalera Tamboril craft identity.
