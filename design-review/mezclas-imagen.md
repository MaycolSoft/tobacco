## Prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset: wide 3:2 editorial still life for a tobacco leaf composition page in a sober educational presentation for adults. A top-down view of three loose cured tobacco leaves, subtly different warm brown tones and natural veins, spaced loosely on a dark worn walnut table. Quiet organic composition, ample clean dark wood in the left third for visual breathing room, leaves concentrated centrally and to the right. Soft warm lateral window light, readable charcoal-green shadows, muted bronze and earth palette. Realistic imperfect leaf textures. No hands, no smoking, no cigars, no drinks, no tools, no logo, no text, no labels, no graphic design. This is atmospheric artwork, not a depiction of a specific botanical variety or verified recipe. Match a refined natural editorial photograph, restrained contrast, no dramatic effects.
